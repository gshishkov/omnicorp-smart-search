# Customer Onboarding Playbook

This playbook describes how CSMs onboard new OmniCorp customers, from contract signature to the first
value milestone. The target is for every new customer to reach "first value" within 30 days.

## Onboarding phases

### Phase 1: Handoff (days 0–3)

The Account Executive completes the sales-to-CS handoff form in the CRM within 2 business days of the
contract signature. The form lists the customer's goals, stakeholders, technical environment and any
commitments made during the sale. The CSM reviews the form and requests clarification within 1 business day.

### Phase 2: Kickoff (days 3–10)

The CSM schedules a 60-minute kickoff call with the customer's executive sponsor and project lead. The
kickoff covers success criteria, timeline, roles and the onboarding plan. Within 24 hours after the call,
the CSM sends a written recap and the agreed success plan.

### Phase 3: Technical setup (days 10–21)

The customer configures workspaces, SSO, integrations and data import with help from the Onboarding
Engineer. Enterprise customers get up to 20 hours of Onboarding Engineer time; Professional customers get
up to 8 hours. Additional hours can be purchased as a professional services package.

### Phase 4: Adoption and first value (days 21–30)

The CSM runs role-based training sessions and tracks activation. "First value" is reached when at least
60% of purchased seats are active in a 7-day period and the customer confirms that the first success
criterion has been met.

## Health score at onboarding

During onboarding, the account health score is based on three signals: seat activation rate, completed
onboarding tasks and stakeholder engagement. An account that has not reached 30% seat activation by day 21
is marked **At Risk**, and the CSM must create a recovery plan with their team lead.

## Handover to steady state

After first value, the CSM schedules the first quarterly business review (QBR) within 90 days of the
contract start date. Onboarding is formally closed when the success plan is updated and the onboarding
survey has been sent to the project lead.

## Common pitfalls

- Starting technical setup before the success criteria are agreed.
- Training only administrators and not end users.
- Missing the executive sponsor in the kickoff call.
