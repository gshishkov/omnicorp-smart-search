# SLA and Support Tiers

OmniCorp offers three support tiers: Standard, Premium and Enterprise. The tier is set in the customer's
order form and applies to every workspace under that contract. This article defines response targets,
severity levels, support channels and how to escalate.

## Support tiers overview

**Standard** is included with every subscription. Support is available by email and the in-app help
center, Monday to Friday, 08:00–18:00 in the customer's contracted region.

**Premium** adds live chat, phone support and 24/7 coverage for Severity 1 incidents. Premium costs
15% of the annual subscription fee.

**Enterprise** includes everything in Premium plus a named Technical Account Manager (TAM), quarterly
business reviews and 24/7 coverage for Severity 1 and Severity 2 incidents. Enterprise support is
included at no extra cost for contracts above USD 250,000 annual contract value (ACV).

## Severity levels

- **Severity 1 (Critical):** the production service is down or unusable for all users, or there is a confirmed data-loss or security incident.
- **Severity 2 (High):** a major feature is broken for many users and no workaround exists.
- **Severity 3 (Normal):** a feature is partially impaired or a workaround exists.
- **Severity 4 (Low):** questions, cosmetic issues and feature requests.

## First response targets

First response targets are measured from ticket creation to the first human reply.

| Severity | Standard | Premium | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Severity 1 | 4 business hours | 1 hour (24/7) | 30 minutes (24/7) |
| Severity 2 | 8 business hours | 4 business hours | 2 hours (24/7) |
| Severity 3 | 2 business days | 1 business day | 8 business hours |
| Severity 4 | 5 business days | 3 business days | 2 business days |

## Uptime commitment and service credits

OmniCorp commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Premium and Enterprise customers. Standard customers have a
99.5% target without service credits. If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, Premium and
Enterprise customers receive a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee for each full 0.1% below the
commitment, capped at 50% of the monthly fee. Customers must request credits within 30 days of the end of
the affected month by opening a billing ticket.

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance on the status page does not count as downtime.

## Escalation path

1. The CSM confirms the ticket severity with the customer and updates it in the support portal.
2. If the response target is missed, the CSM escalates to the Support Duty Manager through the #support-escalations channel.
3. For Severity 1 incidents lasting more than 2 hours, the Support Duty Manager opens an incident bridge and notifies the VP of Customer Success.
4. Enterprise customers receive a written root cause analysis (RCA) within 5 business days of a Severity 1 incident being resolved.
