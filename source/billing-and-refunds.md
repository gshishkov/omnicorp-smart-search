# Billing and Refunds Policy

This policy explains billing cycles, payment terms, how refunds and credits work, and what CSMs can
approve without Finance involvement.

## Billing cycles and payment terms

Customers are billed annually in advance by default. Monthly billing is available only for the Starter
plan. Invoices are issued on the contract start date and on each anniversary.

Payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Enterprise contracts may negotiate net 45 or net 60
terms; any terms longer than net 60 require approval from the CFO.

Accepted payment methods are credit card, ACH bank transfer and wire transfer. Credit card payments are
only accepted for invoices under USD 50,000.

## Late payments

If an invoice is unpaid 15 days after its due date, the billing system sends an automated reminder and the
CSM is notified. At 30 days overdue, the account is flagged as at risk. At 45 days overdue, Finance may
suspend access to the workspace after written notice to the customer's billing contact. CSMs must not
promise to delay a suspension without written approval from the Finance Operations manager.

## Refund eligibility

New customers on an annual plan can cancel within 30 days of the initial contract start date for a full
refund. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are non-refundable, but unused seats can be carried forward as
account credit until the next renewal.

Monthly Starter plans can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing month and no partial-month refunds are issued.

Professional services (onboarding packages, training and custom integrations) are non-refundable once
delivery has started.

## Credits and approvals

CSMs can approve goodwill credits of up to USD 500 per account per quarter without Finance approval.
Credits between USD 500 and USD 5,000 require approval from the CSM's team lead. Credits above USD 5,000
require approval from the Finance Operations manager. All credits must be logged in the billing system
with a reason code.

## Price changes at renewal

Renewal price increases are capped at 7% per year for customers on multi-year agreements. Customers must
be notified of any price change at least 60 days before the renewal date.
